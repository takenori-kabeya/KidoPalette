# KidoPalette Help

KidoPalette is a macOS app launcher. Register the apps you use, group them in tabs, and open the palette from a hot corner.

## Getting started

1. Open the launcher (hot corner, Dock icon if shown, or **Window → Toggle Visibility** / ⌘⇧L).
2. Choose **Add App** and pick `.app` bundles, or drag apps from Finder onto the launcher.
3. Use tabs to group apps. Double-click a tab name to rename it.

## Hot corner

- In **Settings**, choose the trigger corner and whether hot corners are enabled.
- Optionally enable **Show Full Screen** so the launcher covers the display (including above the menu bar and Dock).
- Move the pointer into the configured corner to show or hide the launcher.

## Apps and tabs

- Click an icon to launch the app (the launcher hides afterward).
- Drag icons to reorder them, or drop between icons to insert.
- Drag Finder `.app` items onto empty space after the last icon to append, or onto an insertion gap to insert there.
- Use the context menu to move an app to another tab or remove it.
- Add, rename, reorder, or delete tabs from the tab bar (at least one tab remains).

## Settings

Open Settings from the menu bar (**KidoPalette → Settings…**) or the gear button in the launcher (useful when the Dock icon is hidden).

- Hot corner on/off, corner choice, full-screen presentation
- Show in Dock

## App Sandbox notes

Official builds run under **App Sandbox**. Adding apps via the Open Panel or drag-and-drop grants access; KidoPalette stores security-scoped bookmarks so registered apps keep working after relaunch.
